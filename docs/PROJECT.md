# Baybay

## Overview

Baybay is an audio-first language learning platform that teaches foreign languages through music.

The application combines song excerpts with spoken vocabulary explanations, grammatical instruction, and contextual translations to create continuous, hands-free audio lessons.

## Mission

Make language learning engaging and accessible through music.

## Initial Audience

English speakers learning Spanish who want to improve their vocabulary, grammar, and listening comprehension through music.

## Learning Experience

Each audio lesson follows a structured sequence:

1. Play a segment of a song.
2. Pause the music.
3. Provide concise spoken vocabulary and grammatical explanations.
4. Provide a natural English translation of the lyric.
5. Reinforce the original lyric.
6. Continue to the next segment.

Repeated vocabulary receives shorter explanations to maintain a natural learning pace.

## MVP

The initial release provides a curated catalog of manually prepared Spanish audio lessons through a simple web-based audio player.

The first prototype will use a 30–60-second song excerpt.

## Long-Term Vision

- Automated audio lesson generation.
- Expanded music catalog.
- Additional source and target languages.
- Adjustable instructional depth.
- Personalized language learning experiences.
- Offline and mobile listening.

## Content Rights

All lyrics, recordings, translations, and narration must be original, appropriately licensed, or otherwise authorized for their intended use.
